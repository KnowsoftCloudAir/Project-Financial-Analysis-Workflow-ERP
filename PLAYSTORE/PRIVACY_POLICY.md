This policy explains what Churchgate collects, why we collect it, and how members can control it. It is written to support Google Play Data safety and account-deletion rules.

## 1. Who the app is for
Churchgate is a church management and member app (hierarchy, membership, books/Kwealth, Angel assistant, social/stream panels, memories, subscriptions). It is intended for adults and church communities. If a church enrols minors, the church is responsible for parental consent under applicable law.

## 2. Data we collect
Depending on how you use Churchgate we may process:

- **Account:** name, email, phone (if provided), password (stored hashed), role, church unit  
- **Profile:** photo, membership status, optional testimony text  
- **Church records:** attendance, programmes, messages your church posts  
- **Kwealth / books:** books you upload, reading progress, notes, excerpts  
- **Memories:** photos you upload and optional background music choice  
- **Usage:** pages visited, device/browser type, approximate IP for security and trial limits  
- **Payments / subscriptions:** plan status and payment references (we do not store full card numbers on Churchgate servers)  
- **Support:** emails you send us  

We do **not** sell personal data.

## 3. Why we use data
- Create and secure your account  
- Provide church features you request  
- Read books aloud on-device (speech stays on your device unless you use a cloud voice you choose)  
- Send email or SMS confirmation codes when those services are configured  
- Improve reliability and prevent abuse  
- Comply with law  

Legal bases (where GDPR-style rules apply): contract (providing the service), legitimate interests (security), and consent (optional photos, marketing, SMS).

## 4. Sharing
We share data only with:
- Your church administrators for ministry features you joined  
- Hosting providers (e.g. Render / database host) under contract  
- Payment processors if you subscribe  
- Law enforcement if legally required  

Public social embeds (Facebook / YouTube / TikTok) load content from those platforms under **their** policies when you open a stream.

## 5. Retention
We keep account and church records while the account or church subscription is active. You or General Admin may request deletion. Backups may lag for a short period. Uploaded books and memories are kept until you or an authorised admin delete them.

## 6. Your rights
You may request access, correction, or deletion of your account by emailing **Knowsoftconsult@gmail.com** or using in-app account tools where available. We will respond within a reasonable period.

**Account deletion (Play requirement):** email Knowsoftconsult@gmail.com with subject “Delete Churchgate account” from your registered email. We will delete or anonymise personal account data that is not legally required to keep.

## 7. Children
Churchgate is not directed at children under 13 as a consumer social network. Churches that add children’s ministry records must have appropriate consent.

## 8. Security
Passwords are hashed. Use a unique password. No method is perfect; report issues to Knowsoftconsult@gmail.com.

## 9. International transfer
Servers may be outside Nigeria. By using the app you understand data may be processed in the country of the host.

## 10. Changes
We may update this policy. The effective date above will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

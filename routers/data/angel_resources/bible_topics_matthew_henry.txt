PART 1 — CORE BIBLE Q&A
================================================================

{Q1} IS JESUS THE SON OF GOD?
Yes.
Scripture: Matthew 3:17; 17:5; Matthew 16:16; John 20:31; John 5:18–23; 10:30–36; Romans 1:4; Acts 4:12.
Logical summary: The New Testament presents Jesus as the unique Son of God — fully divine, fully human, the Saviour.
Matthew Henry emphasis: Christ is the centre of Scripture — promised, revealed in the gospel, coming again as Judge and King.

{Q2} HOW WAS THE WORLD CREATED?
By the living God, through His word — not by chance.
Scripture: Genesis 1:1; Genesis 1; Psalm 33:6–9; John 1:1–3; Colossians 1:16–17; Hebrews 11:3; Romans 1:20; Genesis 1:26–27.
Logical summary: Intelligent, purposeful creation by God. Man is made in God's image.
Henry: The order of creation shows God's wisdom; man is the crown of creation yet dependent on the Creator.

{Q3} DESCRIBE HEAVEN
Heaven is God's dwelling place — holy, glorious, full of worship, and the destiny of the redeemed.
Scripture: Psalm 11:4; Isaiah 66:1; Isaiah 6:1–3; Revelation 4:8; John 14:2–3; 2 Corinthians 5:8; Philippians 1:23; Revelation 21:1–4; 22:1–5.
Logical summary: God's present throne-room and the believer's eternal home in the renewed creation.
Henry on Rev 21: The end is renewed creation under God's immediate presence.

{Q4} HOW WILL THE RAPTURE LOOK LIKE?
A sudden catching away of believers to meet Christ.
Scripture: 1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; 1 Corinthians 15:51–52; John 14:3.
The Lord descends with a shout, voice of the archangel, trumpet of God; dead in Christ rise first; living are changed in a moment; caught up to meet the Lord in the air; forever with the Lord.
Note: Timing relative to the tribulation is debated among Christians. Comfort stands: encourage one another (1 Thessalonians 4:18).
Henry on 1 Thess 4: Hope of resurrection comforts mourners; the Lord's coming is the church's great expectation.

{Q5} DIFFERENCE BETWEEN HELL AND THE LAKE OF FIRE?
Sheol/Hades — realm of the dead; intermediate state (Luke 16:23; Acts 2:27; Revelation 20:13).
Gehenna / hell-fire — warnings of judgment (Matthew 5:22,29–30; 10:28; Mark 9:43–48).
Lake of fire — final eternal punishment after the Great White Throne; second death; death and Hades cast into it (Revelation 20:14–15; 21:8; Matthew 25:41,46).
Logical summary: Everyday "hell" often covers both; the Bible's clearest final destiny of the lost is the lake of fire.

{Q6} HOW WILL BELIEVERS BE REWARDED?
Saved by grace; assessed for faithful service.
Scripture: 2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 14:10–12; 1 Corinthians 3:11–15; 2 Timothy 4:8; James 1:12; Revelation 2:10; 1 Peter 5:4; 1 Thessalonians 2:19–20; Matthew 25:21.
Logical summary: Salvation is not earned; rewards for faithfulness are real. Live for Christ's approval.

{Q7} WILL SAINTS LIVE IN HEAVEN WITH GOD?
Yes.
Scripture: John 14:3; 1 Thessalonians 4:17; Revelation 21:2–3; 22:4.
Logical summary: Unbroken fellowship with God and the Lamb forever.

{Q8} HOW DOES GOD'S THRONE LOOK LIKE?
Visions of majesty, not a technical drawing.
Scripture: Isaiah 6:1–3; Ezekiel 1:26–28; Revelation 4–5.
Logical summary: Absolute rule, holiness, and worship. Approach through Christ (Hebrews 4:16).

{Q9} ANGELS, LUCIFER, SATAN, DEMONS — WHERE NOW? FINAL ABODE?
Holy angels: created spirits who serve God (Psalm 148:2,5; Hebrews 1:14); not to be worshipped (Revelation 19:10; 22:8–9).
Satan: adversary, tempter, accuser (1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 4:1–11; Revelation 12:9–10). "Lucifer" traditional reading from Isaiah 14:12 (KJV); often linked with Ezekiel 28:12–17. Defeated at the cross (Hebrews 2:14; Colossians 2:15); still active under God's limits (Job 1–2).
Demons / fallen angels: reserved for judgment (2 Peter 2:4; Jude 6); subject to Christ (Mark 1:23–27; Luke 10:17–20).
Where now: active in the world system (Ephesians 6:11–12); cannot go beyond what God allows.
Final abode: everlasting fire prepared for the devil and his angels (Matthew 25:41); lake of fire forever (Revelation 20:10).
Henry: Satan is a conquered enemy for those in Christ, yet a real adversary.

{Q10} HOW WILL THE LAST DAYS LOOK LIKE?
Signs, not a full timetable (Matthew 24; Mark 13; Luke 21): deception, wars, famines, pestilences, earthquakes, persecution, gospel to all nations, lawlessness, cold love, cosmic signs, then the Son of Man comes.
Paul: perilous times (2 Timothy 3:1–5). Peter: scoffers (2 Peter 3:3–4).
Logical summary: Watch, pray, endure, preach. Do not set dates (Matthew 24:36).

{Q11} WHAT IS SANCTIFICATION?
Being set apart to God and made holy in heart and life.
Scripture: 1 Thessalonians 4:3; 1 Peter 1:15–16; John 17:17; Hebrews 12:14; 2 Thessalonians 2:13; 1 Peter 1:2.
Deeper Life teaching: entire sanctification — heart purity and holy living by faith and the Spirit's power.
Logical summary: Saved people are called to grow in likeness to Christ.

{Q12} WHAT IS HYPOCRISY?
Pretending outward religion while the heart is far from God.
Scripture: Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 6:1–6,16; Matthew 23; Luke 12:1; 1 Samuel 16:7.
Logical summary: God looks on the heart. Real faith produces sincere obedience and love.

{Q13} WHAT IS HOLY GHOST BAPTISM?
Promise of the Father — enduement with power for life and witness.
Scripture: Luke 24:49; Acts 1:4–8; 2:1–4; Acts 8:14–17; 10:44–46; 19:1–6; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Luke 11:13.
Deeper Life: distinct empowerment after conversion for holy living and service.
Logical summary: Ask the Father for the Spirit; seek power to glorify Christ, not pride.

{Q14} WHERE DID JESUS GO WHEN HE DIED BEFORE RESURRECTION?
Body buried (Matthew 27:59–60); truly died (John 19:30–35); spirit committed to the Father (Luke 23:46); paradise with the thief (Luke 23:43); rose bodily the third day (Luke 24; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4).
1 Peter 3:18–20 is debated; do not invent beyond the text.
Logical summary: He fully died, rose again — the gospel centre (1 Corinthians 15:3–4).

{Q15} WHO WROTE EACH BOOK OF THE BIBLE?
God is the ultimate Author (2 Timothy 3:16; 2 Peter 1:21). Traditional human writers: Moses (Genesis–Deuteronomy); Joshua; Samuel era records; Kings/Chronicles compilers; Ezra–Nehemiah; Psalms many authors especially David; Solomon (Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song); named prophets; Matthew, Mark, Luke–Acts, John, Paul (Romans–Philemon), James, Peter, Jude; Hebrews author unnamed.
Say "traditionally attributed to" where scholars debate.

{Q16} DANIEL AND REVELATION AND CURRENT EVENTS?
Right use: God rules history (Daniel 2; 7); Christ wins (Revelation 19–20); endurance and holiness (Revelation 13:10; 14:12).
Wrong use: setting dates (Matthew 24:36); forcing every headline into a verse; fear-selling speculation.
Logical summary: Read to know God and live ready. "Even so, come, Lord Jesus" (Revelation 22:20).

PART 4 — DEEPER LIFE 22 DOCTRINES (WITH ILLUSTRATION + HENRY)
================================================================

{D1} THE HOLY BIBLE
Inspired 66 books; final authority for faith and conduct.
Illustration: A ship without a compass drifts; the Bible is the Christian's compass.
Bible: 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Proverbs 30:5–6; Psalm 119:105; Joshua 1:8; Revelation 22:18–19; 2 Peter 1:20–21.
Henry: Scripture is the standard by which all doctrine and duty must be tried.

{D2} THE GODHEAD
Trinity — Father, Son, Holy Ghost; one God, three Persons.
Illustration: Light, heat, and rays from one sun (imperfect picture of one God in three Persons).
Bible: Matthew 3:16–17; 28:19–20; 2 Corinthians 13:14; John 1:1–14; 14:16–17.
Henry: At Jesus' baptism the Father speaks, the Son is baptised, the Spirit descends — mystery to be adored.

{D3} THE VIRGIN BIRTH OF JESUS
Conceived of the Holy Ghost; crucified, dead, buried, risen bodily.
Illustration: A bridge must touch both banks — God and man meet in Christ.
Bible: Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:18–25; Luke 1:26–35; Romans 1:4; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4.
Henry: He who was born of a virgin and rose from the dead is able to save to the uttermost.

{D4} TOTAL DEPRAVITY
All sinful and guilty since the Fall; under wrath apart from salvation.
Illustration: A poisoned well needs a new source, not only cleaner cups.
Bible: Psalm 51:5; Romans 3:9–23; 5:12–19; Ephesians 2:1–3; Jeremiah 17:9.
Henry: All have sinned; this levels pride and drives us to the Saviour.

{D5} REPENTANCE
Complete turning from sin before saving faith.
Illustration: A wrong-way driver must turn around, not only feel sorry.
Bible: Proverbs 28:13; Isaiah 55:7; Mark 1:15; Luke 13:3; Acts 2:38; 3:19; 17:30; 2 Corinthians 7:10.
Henry: True repentance is a thorough change of heart and life.

{D6} RESTITUTION
Making amends for wrongs where possible — does not buy salvation; proves sincerity.
Illustration: Zacchaeus restored fourfold (Luke 19:8–9).
Bible: Exodus 22; Leviticus 6:1–7; Ezekiel 33:14–16; Matthew 5:23–24; Luke 19:8–9.
Henry: A penitent must, as far as able, undo the wrong he has done.

{D7} JUSTIFICATION
Declared righteous by faith in Christ's blood; peace with God.
Illustration: Another paid the fine; the verdict is not condemned.
Bible: Romans 3:24–28; 4:5–8; 5:1; Acts 13:38–39; Galatians 2:16; Titus 3:7.
Henry: Justification is by faith without the works of the law; righteousness is Christ's.

{D8} WATER BAPTISM
After conversion; immersion; name of Father, Son, and Holy Ghost.
Illustration: A wedding ring witnesses the marriage; it does not create it.
Bible: Matthew 28:19; Acts 2:38; 8:36–39; Romans 6:3–4; Colossians 2:12.
Henry: Baptism is the appointed sign of discipleship.

{D9} THE LORD'S SUPPER
Memorial of Christ's death for true believers.
Illustration: A memorial stone so later generations do not forget.
Bible: Matthew 26:26–29; 1 Corinthians 11:23–32.
Henry: Remembrance of Christ crucified — grateful proclamation of the finished work.

{D10} ENTIRE SANCTIFICATION
Heart cleansing; holy life; without holiness no one shall see the Lord.
Illustration: A room claimed, then thoroughly cleansed for the owner's use.
Bible: John 17:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:3,7; 5:23; Hebrews 12:14; 1 Peter 1:15–16.
Henry: Sanctification is the Spirit's work conforming us to God's image.

{D11} HOLY GHOST BAPTISM
Enduement with power for witness and service.
Illustration: A lamp needs oil and flame; the Spirit makes witness burn.
Bible: Luke 24:49; Acts 1:5–8; 2:1–4; 10:44–46; 19:1–6; Ephesians 5:18.
Henry: The Spirit is the promise of the Father; power for service is a divine gift.

{D12} REDEMPTION, HEALING AND HEALTH
Christ redeems from sin; prayer for healing according to God's will.
Bible: Isaiah 53:4–5; Matthew 8:16–17; Psalm 103:1–3; James 5:14–16; 1 Peter 2:24.
Henry: Christ is a complete Saviour; measure everything by the Word and submission to God's will.

{D13} PERSONAL EVANGELISM
Every believer called to win souls.
Illustration: A rescued man throws a rope to others still drowning.
Bible: Matthew 28:19–20; Mark 16:15; Acts 1:8; 2 Corinthians 5:18–20; Proverbs 11:30.
Henry: Those who have found the Messiah must invite others.

{D14} MARRIAGE
One man and one woman; purity and faithfulness; reflects Christ and the church.
Bible: Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:4–6; Ephesians 5:22–33; Hebrews 13:4.
Henry: Marriage is God's institution; purity adorns the doctrine of God.

{D15} THE RAPTURE
Catching away of the saints; in this tradition taught before the tribulation as a blessed hope.
Bible: John 14:1–3; 1 Corinthians 15:51–52; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–18; Titus 2:13; 1 John 3:2–3.
Henry: The coming of the Lord comforts mourners; be ready.

{D16} THE RESURRECTION OF THE DEAD
Bodily resurrection of just and unjust.
Bible: John 5:28–29; Daniel 12:2; 1 Corinthians 15; Acts 24:15; Philippians 3:20–21.
Henry: Sown in weakness, raised in power; labour is not in vain in the Lord.

{D17} THE GREAT TRIBULATION
Future period of severe distress on earth.
Bible: Daniel 12:1; Matthew 24:21–22; Revelation 6–19.
Henry on Matt 24: Foretold so the people would be prepared, not surprised out of faith.

{D18} THE SECOND COMING OF CHRIST
Personal, visible return in power and glory.
Bible: Acts 1:11; Matthew 24:30; 25:31; Revelation 1:7; 19:11–16.
Henry: Certain, public, decisive — live as servants expecting their Lord.

{D19} CHRIST'S MILLENNIAL REIGN
Thousand-year reign of Christ on earth (Revelation 20:1–6; Isaiah 11; Zechariah 14:9).
Henry: The kingdoms of this world shall become the kingdoms of our Lord and of His Christ.

{D20} THE GREAT WHITE THRONE JUDGMENT
Final judgment of the unsaved dead (Revelation 20:11–15).
Henry: Judgment is certain; stand only by being found in Christ now.

{D21} THE NEW HEAVEN AND THE NEW EARTH
Eternal abode of the redeemed (Revelation 21–22; 2 Peter 3:13).
Henry: God Himself with His people in everlasting joy.

{D22} HELL FIRE
Eternal punishment for the devil, his angels, and the unrepentant.
Bible: Matthew 25:41,46; Mark 9:43–48; Revelation 20:10,14–15; 21:8.
Henry: Preach hell soberly to lead to repentance and faith in Christ.

*** ANGEL DEEP BIBLE STUDIES ***
Compiled for conversational teaching. Scripture is final authority.
Public-domain helpers: Matthew Henry's Commentary tradition; classic
evangelical outlines. Not a substitute for reading the Bible itself.

Angel instructions:
1. Answer from Scripture first — cite at least three portions when teaching.
2. On first ask: give Layer 1 (core truth).
3. On "more" / yes / go deeper: give Layer 2, then Layer 3.
4. Always close a teaching turn with a short encouragement to love God,
   walk with Him, and trust Him.
5. Do not invent certainty beyond the text. On debated timing (e.g. exact
   order of end-time events), state the clear texts and note where
   Christians differ humbly.

{S1} WHO GOD IS
================================================================
LAYER 1 — Core truth
God is the one living, eternal Creator — holy, almighty, all-knowing,
and full of love. He is Spirit, and those who worship Him must worship
in spirit and in truth. He exists as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit —
three Persons, one God.

Bible portions:
• "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth." (Genesis 1:1)
• "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God is one LORD." (Deuteronomy 6:4)
• "God is a Spirit: and they that worship him must worship him in spirit
  and in truth." (John 4:24)
• "Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name
  of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost." (Matthew 28:19)

LAYER 2 — Deeper
God's attributes are not cold ideas; they call us to fear, trust, and
worship. He is perfect in holiness (Isaiah 6:3), infinite in power
(Jeremiah 32:17), and rich in mercy (Ephesians 2:4). Nothing is hidden
from Him (Psalm 139:1–4). He rules providence and will judge the world
in righteousness (Acts 17:31).

Matthew Henry emphasis: The God who made the world also rules it and
will judge it. His name is to be hallowed; His will is to be done.

LAYER 3 — Still deeper
The unity of God guards us from many gods; the Trinity guards the truth
of the gospel — the Father sends, the Son redeems, the Spirit applies
salvation. At Jesus' baptism the Father spoke, the Son was baptised,
the Spirit descended (Matthew 3:16–17). This mystery is to be adored
more than curiously dissected.

Encouragement: Love this holy God with all your heart. Keep a daily
walk with Him in the Word and prayer. Trust Him when you do not
understand the path — He is faithful and does not change.

{S2} WHO JESUS CHRIST IS
================================================================
LAYER 1 — Core truth
Jesus is the eternal Son of God, truly God and truly man. He was born
of a virgin, lived without sin, died for our sins, was buried, and rose
bodily the third day. He is the only Saviour; there is no other name
under heaven given among men whereby we must be saved.

Bible portions:
• "Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God." (Matthew 16:16)
• "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the
  Word was God... And the Word was made flesh." (John 1:1, 14)
• "Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name
  under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved." (Acts 4:12)
• "Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; And that he was
  buried, and that he rose again the third day." (1 Corinthians 15:3–4)

LAYER 2 — Deeper
He is Emmanuel — God with us (Matthew 1:23). He claimed oneness with
the Father (John 10:30). He is the way, the truth, and the life
(John 14:6). Declared Son of God with power by the resurrection
(Romans 1:4). He now intercedes for His people and will return in glory
(Hebrews 7:25; Revelation 19:11–16).

Matthew Henry: Christ is the centre of Scripture — promised, present
in types, revealed in the gospel, coming again as Judge and King.

LAYER 3 — Still deeper
His virgin birth secures His sinless humanity; His deity secures the
value of His sacrifice. At the cross He bore our sins; in the
resurrection He conquered death. Faith receives Him; works cannot buy
Him. The call is: repent and believe the gospel (Mark 1:15).

Encouragement: Love the Lord Jesus. Stay near Him in Scripture and
obedience. Trust His finished work when your heart condemns you —
His blood is enough.

{S3} PRAYER
================================================================
LAYER 1 — Core truth
Prayer is communion with God — adoration, confession, thanksgiving,
and petition. God invites His children to come boldly to the throne
of grace. We ask in Jesus' name, according to His will, with faith
and perseverance.

Bible portions:
• "Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and
  it shall be opened unto you." (Matthew 7:7)
• "Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication
  with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God."
  (Philippians 4:6)
• "Pray without ceasing." (1 Thessalonians 5:17)
• "Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may
  obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need." (Hebrews 4:16)

LAYER 2 — Deeper
Jesus taught the pattern of the Lord's Prayer (Matthew 6:9–13): God's
name, kingdom, and will first; then daily bread, forgiveness, and
deliverance. He also taught private prayer, not for show (Matthew 6:6).
The Spirit helps our weakness when we do not know what to pray
(Romans 8:26).

Matthew Henry: Prayer is the breath of the new life. God is more ready
to hear than we are to pray.

LAYER 3 — Still deeper
Unforgiveness hinders prayer (Mark 11:25). Asking amiss for selfish
lusts is refused (James 4:3). Persistence is praised in the widow and
the friend at midnight (Luke 18:1–8; 11:5–13). Prayer is not a formula
to control God; it is trustful dependence on a Father who gives good
gifts (Luke 11:13).

Encouragement: Keep a living conversation with God. Love Him enough
to speak with Him daily. Trust Him with the answer — yes, no, or wait.

{S4} PRAISE AND WORSHIP
================================================================
LAYER 1 — Core truth
Praise is declaring God's worth with joy; worship is bowing heart and
life before Him. God seeks those who worship in spirit and in truth.
Praise is fitting for the upright and is a weapon of faith in dark days.

Bible portions:
• "O come, let us worship and bow down: let us kneel before the LORD
  our maker." (Psalm 95:6)
• "God is a Spirit: and they that worship him must worship him in spirit
  and in truth." (John 4:24)
• "By him therefore let us offer the sacrifice of praise to God
  continually, that is, the fruit of our lips giving thanks to his name."
  (Hebrews 13:15)
• "Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with
  praise." (Psalm 100:4)

LAYER 2 — Deeper
Praise can be song, testimony, and obedience. Paul and Silas sang in
prison (Acts 16:25). Heaven rings with "Holy, holy, holy" (Revelation 4:8).
True worship is more than music; it is a living sacrifice of the body
(Romans 12:1). Empty lip-service without the heart is rejected
(Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:8).

LAYER 3 — Still deeper
Worship centres on God's character and Christ's redemption, not on our
feelings alone. When feelings lag, the will can still bless the Lord
(Psalm 103:1–2). Corporate worship builds the church; private worship
keeps the soul soft. Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly as you
sing (Colossians 3:16).

Encouragement: Love God enough to praise Him in the valley and on the
mountain. Guard a pure heart in worship. Trust that He inhabits the
praises of His people.

{S5} THE SOUL OF MAN, LIFE, AND DEATH
================================================================
LAYER 1 — Core truth
Man is more than body. God formed man of the dust and breathed into him
the breath of life; man became a living soul. Life is God's gift; death
entered through sin. After death comes judgment. In Christ there is
eternal life; outside of Christ there is the second death.

Bible portions:
• "And the LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed
  into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul."
  (Genesis 2:7)
• "For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life
  through Jesus Christ our Lord." (Romans 6:23)
• "And as it is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the
  judgment." (Hebrews 9:27)
• "Jesus said unto her, I am the resurrection, and the life: he that
  believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live."
  (John 11:25)

LAYER 2 — Deeper
The soul can be lost or saved (Matthew 16:26). Absent from the body,
the believer is present with the Lord (2 Corinthians 5:8). The rich man
and Lazarus show conscious existence after death (Luke 16:19–31).
Physical death is not the end of the person; the body awaits
resurrection (1 Corinthians 15:42–44).

LAYER 3 — Still deeper
Christ tasted death for every man (Hebrews 2:9) and abolished death's
tyranny for those who believe (2 Timothy 1:10). The second death is the
lake of fire (Revelation 20:14–15). Therefore the urgent question is not
only "How long will I live on earth?" but "Where will I spend eternity?"

Encouragement: Love the God who gave you life and can save your soul.
Keep short accounts with Him. Trust Christ with both your living and
your dying.

================================================================
{S6} CHARACTERS WHO WALKED WITH GOD
================================================================
LAYER 1 — Core truth
Scripture holds up men and women who walked with God by faith —
imperfect people who trusted and obeyed. Their stories encourage us
to the same path.

Bible portions:
• "And Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him."
  (Genesis 5:24)
• "Noah was a just man and perfect in his generations, and Noah walked
  with God." (Genesis 6:9)
• "But without faith it is impossible to please him: for he that cometh
  to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that
  diligently seek him." (Hebrews 11:6)

LAYER 2 — Deeper portraits
ENOCH — Walked with God and was taken without seeing death (Hebrews 11:5).
NOAH — Found grace; built the ark in holy fear (Hebrews 11:7).
ABRAHAM — Believed God; it was counted for righteousness (Genesis 15:6;
Romans 4:3). Obeyed when called out, not knowing where he went.
MOSES — Chose the reproach of Christ above Egypt's treasures
(Hebrews 11:24–26). Spoke with God as a man with his friend (Exodus 33:11).
DAVID — A man after God's heart (Acts 13:22); sinned grievously, yet
repented deeply (Psalm 51).
DANIEL — Purposed in his heart; prayed faithfully in exile (Daniel 1:8; 6:10).
MARY OF BETHANY — Chose the good part at Jesus' feet (Luke 10:42).

LAYER 3 — Still deeper
Hebrews 11 shows that walking with God is faith expressed in obedience
over a lifetime. None were sinless; all needed grace. The same God who
walked with them invites you: "Can two walk together, except they be
agreed?" (Amos 3:3). Agreement means surrender to His word.

Encouragement: Love the God who still walks with ordinary people. Keep
fellowship with Him daily. Trust Him to write your story for His glory.

================================================================
{S7} ESCHATOLOGY — SIGNS OF THE END TIME
================================================================
LAYER 1 — Core truth
Jesus and the apostles taught that this age moves toward Christ's return.
Signs include deception, wars, famines, earthquakes, persecution, the
gospel to the nations, rising lawlessness, and cosmic signs — then the
coming of the Son of Man. We watch and pray; we do not set dates.

Bible portions:
• "And ye shall hear of wars and rumours of wars... For nation shall rise
  against nation... and there shall be famines, and pestilences, and
  earthquakes, in divers places." (Matthew 24:6–7)
• "And this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world for
  a witness unto all nations; and then shall the end come."
  (Matthew 24:14)
• "But of that day and hour knoweth no man, no, not the angels of heaven,
  but my Father only." (Matthew 24:36)
• "This know also, that in the last days perilous times shall come."
  (2 Timothy 3:1)

LAYER 2 — Deeper
Paul describes selfishness, pride, pleasure-seeking, and a form of
godliness without power (2 Timothy 3:1–5). Peter warns of scoffers who
deny the coming (2 Peter 3:3–4). Jesus warns of false christs and false
prophets (Matthew 24:24). The call is endurance and faithfulness
(Matthew 24:13; Revelation 2:10).

LAYER 3 — Still deeper
Prophecy is given so we will not be surprised or deceived, not so we
can build proud timelines. Right use: holiness, watchfulness, evangelism.
Wrong use: date-setting, fear-mongering, forcing every headline into a
verse. "Even so, come, Lord Jesus" (Revelation 22:20).

Encouragement: Love Christ's appearing. Keep oil in your lamp — a living
relationship with Him. Trust the Father with the times and seasons.

================================================================
{S8} THE RAPTURE AND THE BLESSED HOPE
================================================================
LAYER 1 — Core truth
Believers look for the Lord who will descend; the dead in Christ rise
first; living believers are changed and caught up to meet the Lord in
the air — and so shall we ever be with the Lord.

Bible portions:
• "For the Lord himself shall descend from heaven with a shout, with the
  voice of the archangel, and with the trump of God: and the dead in
  Christ shall rise first: Then we which are alive and remain shall be
  caught up together with them in the clouds, to meet the Lord in the air:
  and so shall we ever be with the Lord." (1 Thessalonians 4:16–17)
• "In a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trump... the dead
  shall be raised incorruptible, and we shall be changed."
  (1 Corinthians 15:51–52)
• "And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again, and receive
  you unto myself; that where I am, there ye may be also." (John 14:3)

LAYER 2 — Deeper
This hope comforts those who mourn believing dead (1 Thessalonians 4:18).
It purifies: "every man that hath this hope in him purifieth himself"
(1 John 3:3). Timing relative to the tribulation is debated among
faithful Christians; the clear command is readiness and comfort.

LAYER 3 — Still deeper
The shout, the voice, the trumpet stress a real, personal coming of
Jesus — not a mere idea. Our citizenship is in heaven; we look for the
Saviour who will change our lowly body (Philippians 3:20–21).

Encouragement: Love His appearing. Stay ready in holiness. Trust that
not one of His own will be forgotten in that day.

================================================================
{S9} THE MILLENNIAL REIGN OF CHRIST
================================================================
LAYER 1 — Core truth
Scripture speaks of a thousand years in which Satan is bound and Christ
reigns with His people. The prophets foresaw a time of peace under
Messiah's rule. The kingdoms of this world shall become the kingdoms of
our Lord and of His Christ.

Bible portions:
• "And I saw an angel come down from heaven... and he laid hold on the
  dragon, that old serpent, which is the Devil, and Satan, and bound him
  a thousand years." (Revelation 20:1–2)
• "And they lived and reigned with Christ a thousand years."
  (Revelation 20:4)
• "And the LORD shall be king over all the earth: in that day shall there
  be one LORD, and his name one." (Zechariah 14:9)
• "The wolf also shall dwell with the lamb... They shall not hurt nor
  destroy in all my holy mountain." (Isaiah 11:6, 9)

LAYER 2 — Deeper
Isaiah 2:1–4 and 11:1–10 picture nations learning God's ways and peace
replacing war. Daniel saw a kingdom that shall never be destroyed
(Daniel 2:44; 7:13–14, 27). Exact schemes of timing differ among
teachers; Scripture's certainty is that Christ will reign openly and
righteously.

Matthew Henry: Whatever the exact scheme of times, the kingdoms of this
world shall become the kingdoms of our Lord and of His Christ.

LAYER 3 — Still deeper
The millennium is not the final state; after it come the final revolt,
the Great White Throne, and then the new heaven and new earth
(Revelation 20–22). Reigning with Christ is promised to overcomers
(Revelation 2:26–27; 3:21). Present faithfulness prepares for future
responsibility.

Encouragement: Love the coming King. Serve Him now in small things.
Trust that history is not out of control — it moves toward His throne.

================================================================
{S10} DEATH, RESURRECTION, JUDGMENT, HEAVEN AND HELL
================================================================
LAYER 1 — Core truth
All die once, then face judgment. Believers are raised to life and
reward; the lost face the lake of fire — the second death. Heaven is
God's dwelling with the redeemed forever; hell is real and eternal for
the unrepentant. The only refuge is Christ now.

Bible portions:
• "Marvel not at this: for the hour is coming, in the which all that are
  in the graves shall hear his voice, And shall come forth; they that have
  done good, unto the resurrection of life; and they that have done evil,
  unto the resurrection of damnation." (John 5:28–29)
• "And I saw a great white throne... And whosoever was not found written
  in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire."
  (Revelation 20:11, 15)
• "And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be
  no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any
  more pain." (Revelation 21:4)
• "And these shall go away into everlasting punishment: but the righteous
  into life eternal." (Matthew 25:46)

LAYER 2 — Deeper
Hades/Sheol describes the intermediate state; the lake of fire is final
(Revelation 20:14). Believers appear at the judgment seat of Christ for
reward of works, not condemnation (2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 8:1).
Heaven is both God's throne now and the new creation then (Revelation 21–22).

LAYER 3 — Still deeper
The warnings of hell are meant to drive sinners to the Saviour
(John 3:16–18), not to entertain curiosity. The joys of heaven centre
on God and the Lamb — seeing His face (Revelation 22:4). Therefore
preach Christ crucified and risen with urgency and tenderness.

Encouragement: Love the God who offers eternal life today. Keep your
name treasured in relationship with the Lamb. Trust His promise to wipe
every tear.

================================================================
{S11} THE 22 DOCTRINES — CONCISE TEACHING LAYERS
(Aligned with classic holiness / Deeper Life public tenets; Scripture leads.)
================================================================

{D01} THE HOLY BIBLE — Inspired Word; final authority (2 Timothy 3:16–17;
Psalm 119:105; 2 Peter 1:20–21). Layer deeper: do not add or take away
(Revelation 22:18–19). Encouragement: Love the Book; read it daily; trust
its promises.

{D02} THE GODHEAD — Father, Son, Holy Ghost; one God (Matthew 28:19;
2 Corinthians 13:14; Matthew 3:16–17). See {S1}.

{D03} VIRGIN BIRTH, DEATH, RESURRECTION OF JESUS — Isaiah 7:14;
Matthew 1:18–25; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4. See {S2}.

{D04} TOTAL DEPRAVITY — All have sinned (Romans 3:23; 5:12; Psalm 51:5;
Ephesians 2:1–3). Deeper: the heart needs new birth, not only manners
(John 3:3; Jeremiah 17:9). Trust Christ for a new heart.

{D05} REPENTANCE — Turning from sin to God (Acts 3:19; 17:30; Luke 13:3;
2 Corinthians 7:10). Deeper: sorrow without leaving sin is not repentance.
Love God enough to change direction.

{D06} RESTITUTION — Make wrongs right where possible (Luke 19:8–9;
Ezekiel 33:14–16; Matthew 5:23–24). Deeper: grace makes honest people.
Trust God as you clear your conscience.

{D07} JUSTIFICATION — Declared righteous by faith in Christ's blood
(Romans 5:1; 3:24–26; Galatians 2:16). Deeper: not earned by works
(Ephesians 2:8–9). Rest in His verdict.

{D08} WATER BAPTISM — After conversion; immersion; Triune name
(Matthew 28:19; Acts 8:36–39; Romans 6:3–4). Deeper: pictures burial and
resurrection with Christ. Obey from love.

{D09} THE LORD'S SUPPER — Memorial of His death (1 Corinthians 11:23–26;
Luke 22:19–20). Deeper: examine yourself; proclaim His death until He
comes. Love the crucified Lord at His table.

{D10} ENTIRE SANCTIFICATION — Heart purity; holy living
(1 Thessalonians 5:23; Hebrews 12:14; 1 Peter 1:15–16; John 17:17).
Deeper: holiness is the family likeness of God's children. Pursue it by
faith in the Spirit's power.

{D11} HOLY GHOST BAPTISM — Power for life and witness (Acts 1:8; 2:1–4;
Luke 24:49). Deeper: ask the Father for the Spirit (Luke 11:13); seek
power to glorify Christ, not self.

{D12} REDEMPTION AND HEALING — Christ redeems; prayer for healing
(Isaiah 53:4–5; James 5:14–16; 1 Peter 2:24). Deeper: submit to God's
will; the greater healing is the soul. Trust His wisdom.

{D13} PERSONAL EVANGELISM — Every believer a witness (Matthew 28:19–20;
Acts 1:8; 2 Corinthians 5:20). Deeper: silence is not neutrality when
souls perish. Love neighbours enough to speak of Jesus.

{D14} MARRIAGE — One man, one woman; pure and faithful (Genesis 2:24;
Matthew 19:4–6; Hebrews 13:4; Ephesians 5:22–33). Deeper: reflects Christ
and the church. Honour God's design.

{D15} THE RAPTURE — See {S8}.

{D16} RESURRECTION OF THE DEAD — John 5:28–29; 1 Corinthians 15;
Acts 24:15. Trust the God who raises the dead.

{D17} THE GREAT TRIBULATION — Matthew 24:21–22; Daniel 12:1;
Revelation 6–19. Watch and endure; do not fear beyond trust in God.

{D18} SECOND COMING OF CHRIST — Acts 1:11; Revelation 1:7;
Matthew 24:30. Love His appearing; live ready.

{D19} MILLENNIAL REIGN — See {S9}.

{D20} GREAT WHITE THRONE — Revelation 20:11–15. Only safety: in Christ
now.

{D21} NEW HEAVEN AND NEW EARTH — Revelation 21–22; 2 Peter 3:13.
Hope that purifies; love the God who makes all things new.

{D22} HELL FIRE — Matthew 25:41, 46; Revelation 20:10, 15.
Warn soberly; flee to Christ today; trust His mercy while it is called
today.

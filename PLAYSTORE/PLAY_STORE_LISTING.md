## Full description
Churchgate helps congregations stay connected.

• Member space for your Global → District church  
• Kwealth books: upload PDF/text, listen with voice, full-screen reading  
• Angel voice assistant for church tools (status, books, streams)  
• Memories slideshow with your photos and music  
• Hymns, notes, pastor messages and member interaction  
• Invite & earn (where enabled by your church admin)

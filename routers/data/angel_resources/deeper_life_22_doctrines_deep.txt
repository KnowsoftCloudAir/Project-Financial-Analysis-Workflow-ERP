*** ANGEL DEEP STUDY — DEEPER LIFE 22 BIBLE DOCTRINES ***
Expanded teaching layers for Angel (go deeper and deeper).
Scripture is final authority. Public statements of Deeper Christian
Life Ministry / Deeper Life Bible Church list TWENTY-TWO doctrines
(not twenty-three). This file expands all 22 thoroughly.

Angel rules:
1. Start with LAYER 1 when first asked.
2. On "more", "yes", "go on", "explain" → LAYER 2, then 3, then 4.
3. Quote Scripture freely (at least three portions in full answers).
4. End each teaching turn by encouraging love for God, a pure walk
   with Him, and trust in Him through Jesus Christ.
5. Do not invent beyond the Bible. On timing debates, state clear texts
   and note where sincere believers differ.

{DL01} THE HOLY BIBLE
================================================================
LAYER 1
The Holy Bible — 39 Old Testament and 27 New Testament books — is the
inspired Word of God. It is the final authority for Christian faith,
conduct, and work. No vision, tradition, or opinion stands above it.

Bible portions:
• "All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for
  doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness:
  That the man of God may be perfect, throughly furnished unto all good
  works." (2 Timothy 3:16–17)
• "Every word of God is pure: he is a shield unto them that put their
  trust in him. Add thou not unto his words, lest he reprove thee, and
  thou be found a liar." (Proverbs 30:5–6)
• "Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path."
  (Psalm 119:105)
• "For the prophecy came not in old time by the will of man: but holy
  men of God spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost."
  (2 Peter 1:21)

LAYER 2
Inspiration means God breathed out the Scriptures. They are sufficient
to save, to equip, and to judge every teaching (John 12:48). Jesus
treated Moses, the Prophets, and the Psalms as God's word (Luke 24:44).
We must not add or take away (Revelation 22:18–19). Joshua was told not
to let the book of the law depart from his mouth (Joshua 1:8).

LAYER 3
The Bible exposes the heart (Hebrews 4:12). It produces faith
(Romans 10:17). It sanctifies (John 17:17). Neglect of the Word produces
weakness; meditation produces strength. Compare every preacher, dream,
and tradition with the written Word like the Bereans (Acts 17:11).

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: Scripture is the standard by which all doctrine and duty
must be tried. Practical path: read daily, obey promptly, teach faithfully,
and refuse any gospel that contradicts the apostles (Galatians 1:8–9).

Encouragement: Love the Book of God. Keep it open in your life. Trust
every promise He has spoken — heaven and earth shall pass away, but His
words shall not pass away (Matthew 24:35).

{DL02} THE GODHEAD (TRINITY)
================================================================
LAYER 1
The Godhead consists of three distinct Persons — the Father, the Son,
and the Holy Ghost — perfectly united in one God. Not three gods, and
not merely three names for one Person.

Bible portions:
• "Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of
  the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost." (Matthew 28:19)
• "The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the
  communion of the Holy Ghost, be with you all." (2 Corinthians 13:14)
• "And Jesus, when he was baptized, went up straightway out of the water:
  and, lo, the heavens were opened unto him, and he saw the Spirit of God
  descending like a dove, and lighting upon him: And lo a voice from
  heaven, saying, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased."
  (Matthew 3:16–17)
• "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God is one LORD." (Deuteronomy 6:4)

LAYER 2
The Father plans, the Son redeems, the Spirit applies. Creation involves
all three (Genesis 1:1–2; John 1:1–3; Job 33:4). Salvation involves all
three (Ephesians 1:3–14). Prayer is to the Father, through the Son, in
the Spirit (Ephesians 2:18).

LAYER 3
Denying the Son is denying the Father (1 John 2:22–23). The Spirit is a
Person who can be grieved (Ephesians 4:30), lies can be told to Him
(Acts 5:3–4). Worship belongs to God alone — Father, Son, and Spirit.

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: At Jesus' baptism three Persons appear as one Godhead.
This mystery is to be adored more than curiously dissected. Live in
fellowship with the triune God in prayer and holiness.

Encouragement: Love the Father who sent the Son. Love the Son who gave
Himself. Love the Spirit who dwells within. Trust the one true God with
all your heart.

{DL03} THE VIRGIN BIRTH OF JESUS (AND HIS DEATH, BURIAL, RESURRECTION)
================================================================
LAYER 1
Jesus Christ, the only begotten Son of God, was conceived of the Holy
Ghost and born of the virgin Mary. He was crucified, truly died, was
buried, and rose bodily the third day. This is the heart of the gospel.

Bible portions:
• "Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his
  name Immanuel." (Isaiah 7:14)
• "Now the birth of Jesus Christ was on this wise: When as his mother
  Mary was espoused to Joseph, before they came together, she was found
  with child of the Holy Ghost." (Matthew 1:18)
• "For I delivered unto you first of all that which I also received, how
  that Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; And that he
  was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the
  scriptures." (1 Corinthians 15:3–4)
• "And declared to be the Son of God with power... by the resurrection
  from the dead." (Romans 1:4)

LAYER 2
Without the virgin birth, His humanity would be under Adam's sin-stream;
without the cross, no atonement; without the resurrection, faith is vain
(1 Corinthians 15:14–17). He is fully God and fully man (John 1:1, 14;
Colossians 2:9). He ascended and will return (Acts 1:9–11).

LAYER 3
The empty tomb is historical gospel fact attested by many witnesses
(1 Corinthians 15:5–8). His blood cleanses from all sin (1 John 1:7).
His priesthood is unchangeable (Hebrews 7:24–25). Rejecting this gospel
leaves a person still in their sins.

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: He who was born of a virgin and rose from the dead is
able to save to the uttermost. Embrace Him by faith; confess Him as Lord.

Encouragement: Love the crucified and risen Jesus. Keep your faith fixed
on His finished work. Trust Him alone for salvation and daily grace.

{DL04} TOTAL DEPRAVITY, SINFULNESS AND GUILT OF ALL MEN
================================================================
LAYER 1
Since Adam's fall, all people are sinful by nature and by choice, guilty
before God, and under wrath apart from salvation. No one is righteous by
self-effort.

Bible portions:
• "Behold, I was shapen in iniquity; and in sin did my mother conceive me."
  (Psalm 51:5)
• "For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God." (Romans 3:23)
• "Wherefore, as by one man sin entered into the world, and death by sin;
  and so death passed upon all men, for that all have sinned."
  (Romans 5:12)
• "And you hath he quickened, who were dead in trespasses and sins."
  (Ephesians 2:1)
• "For from within, out of the heart of men, proceed evil thoughts..."
  (Mark 7:21–23)

LAYER 2
Sin is not only bad actions; it is a bent of the heart away from God
(Jeremiah 17:9). Job asked, "Who can bring a clean thing out of an
unclean?" (Job 14:4). The law stops every mouth (Romans 3:19). Good
works cannot cancel guilt (Isaiah 64:6).

LAYER 3
Total depravity does not mean everyone is as wicked as possible; it means
every part of the person is affected — mind, will, emotions — so that
without grace none seeks God as they ought (Romans 3:10–12). New birth is
necessary (John 3:3–7).

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: This levels pride and drives us to the Saviour. Admit your
need; do not defend your sin; run to Christ.

Encouragement: Love the God who still seeks sinners. Keep a soft heart
that confesses quickly. Trust the blood of Jesus to cleanse every stain.

{DL05} REPENTANCE
================================================================
LAYER 1
Repentance is a complete turning away from sin unto God. It is required
in the gospel call before and with saving faith in Jesus Christ. Without
repentance there is no true conversion.

Bible portions:
• "Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted
  out." (Acts 3:19)
• "And the times of this ignorance God winked at; but now commandeth all
  men every where to repent." (Acts 17:30)
• "I tell you, Nay: but, except ye repent, ye shall all likewise perish."
  (Luke 13:3)
• "For godly sorrow worketh repentance to salvation not to be repented of:
  but the sorrow of the world worketh death." (2 Corinthians 7:10)
• "He that covereth his sins shall not prosper: but whoso confesseth and
  forsaketh them shall have mercy." (Proverbs 28:13)

LAYER 2
Repentance is more than tears or regret. It is a change of mind that
produces a change of life (Isaiah 55:7; Ezekiel 18:21–23). John the
Baptist demanded fruits worthy of repentance (Luke 3:8). Jesus linked
repentance and faith (Mark 1:15).

LAYER 3
Restitution often follows true repentance (see DL06). Ongoing repentance
marks a healthy believer (1 John 1:9). Hardening the heart against
conviction is dangerous (Hebrews 3:13–15).

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: True repentance is a thorough change of heart and life.
Sorrow without leaving sin is not repentance unto life.

Encouragement: Love God enough to turn from what grieves Him. Keep short
accounts — confess and forsake. Trust His mercy; He receives the humble.

{DL06} RESTITUTION
================================================================
LAYER 1
Restitution means making amends for wrongs done — restoring stolen goods,
paying debts, correcting lies, and apologising where possible. It does
not purchase salvation; it proves a sincere break with the old life.

Bible portions:
• "And Zacchaeus stood, and said unto the Lord; Behold, Lord, the half of
  my goods I give to the poor; and if I have taken any thing from any man
  by false accusation, I restore him fourfold." (Luke 19:8)
• "If the wicked restore the pledge, give again that he had robbed, walk
  in the statutes of life, without committing iniquity; he shall surely
  live, he shall not die." (Ezekiel 33:15)
• "Therefore if thou bring thy gift to the altar, and there rememberest
  that thy brother hath ought against thee; Leave there thy gift... first
  be reconciled to thy brother." (Matthew 5:23–24)
• "Speak unto the children of Israel, When a man or woman shall commit any
  sin that men commit... then they shall confess their sin which they have
  done: and he shall recompense his trespass with the principal thereof,
  and add unto it the fifth part thereof." (Numbers 5:6–7)

LAYER 2
Old Testament law required restoration plus addition in some cases
(Exodus 22:1–4; Leviticus 6:1–5). Grace does not cancel honesty; it
produces it. Some wrongs cannot be fully undone; do what is possible and
leave the rest with God.

LAYER 3
Pride delays restitution; love hastens it. A clear conscience toward men
adorns the gospel (Acts 24:16). Seek wise counsel when the case is complex.

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: A penitent must, as far as able, undo the wrong he has
done. Practical holiness includes financial and relational integrity.

Encouragement: Love truth more than reputation. Keep your hands clean.
Trust God to help you make things right step by step.

{DL07} JUSTIFICATION
================================================================
LAYER 1
Justification is God's gracious act of declaring the believing sinner
righteous through faith in the blood of Jesus Christ. Sins are forgiven;
peace with God begins. It is not earned by works of the law.

Bible portions:
• "Therefore being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our
  Lord Jesus Christ." (Romans 5:1)
• "Being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in
  Christ Jesus: Whom God hath set forth to be a propitiation through faith
  in his blood." (Romans 3:24–25)
• "Knowing that a man is not justified by the works of the law, but by the
  faith of Jesus Christ." (Galatians 2:16)
• "Be it known unto you therefore, men and brethren, that through this man
  is preached unto you the forgiveness of sins: And by him all that believe
  are justified from all things." (Acts 13:38–39)

LAYER 2
Abraham was counted righteous by faith before circumcision (Romans 4:3–5).
Works follow justification; they do not cause it (Ephesians 2:8–10). The
righteousness is Christ's; the receiving hand is faith.

LAYER 3
Justification answers guilt; sanctification addresses the heart's pollution
(see DL10). The justified must not continue in sin (Romans 6:1–2). Assurance
grows as we look to Christ, not to ourselves (Romans 8:1, 33–34).

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: Justification is by faith without the works of the law.
Rest in the verdict of the Judge who justifies the ungodly who believe.

Encouragement: Love the Justifier. Keep trusting His blood, not your
record. Stand firm in the peace He purchased.

{DL08} WATER BAPTISM
================================================================
LAYER 1
Water baptism is for those who have already repented and believed. It is
by immersion in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Ghost. It publicly
pictures burial with Christ and rising to new life. It is obedience, not
the price of salvation.

Bible portions:
• "Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of
  the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost." (Matthew 28:19)
• "And as they went on their way, they came unto a certain water: and the
  eunuch said, See, here is water; what doth hinder me to be baptized?
  And Philip said, If thou believest with all thine heart, thou mayest."
  (Acts 8:36–37)
• "Therefore we are buried with him by baptism into death: that like as
  Christ was raised up from the dead by the glory of the Father, even so
  we also should walk in newness of life." (Romans 6:3–4)
• "Buried with him in baptism, wherein also ye are risen with him through
  the faith of the operation of God." (Colossians 2:12)

LAYER 2
New Testament examples show believers baptised after conversion
(Acts 2:38–41; 10:47–48; 16:30–33). Immersion matches the meaning of
burial and rising. Infant christening without personal faith is not the
New Testament pattern.

LAYER 3
Baptism does not regenerate; faith and the Spirit do (John 3:5–8;
Titus 3:5). Yet refusing baptism when able is refusing a clear command of
Christ. It is a mark of discipleship.

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: Baptism is the appointed sign of discipleship. It binds
us openly to the triune God. Obey promptly after true conversion.

Encouragement: Love Christ enough to confess Him openly. Keep walking in
the newness of life your baptism declared. Trust His grace daily.

{DL09} THE LORD'S SUPPER
================================================================
LAYER 1
The Lord's Supper (Holy Communion) is a memorial of Christ's death. The
bread and the cup remind believers of His body and blood given for us.
It is for true believers in fellowship, who examine themselves and
proclaim the Lord's death until He comes.

Bible portions:
• "And he took bread, and gave thanks, and brake it, and gave unto them,
  saying, This is my body which is given for you: this do in remembrance
  of me." (Luke 22:19)
• "For as often as ye eat this bread, and drink this cup, ye do shew the
  Lord's death till he come." (1 Corinthians 11:26)
• "But let a man examine himself, and so let him eat of that bread, and
  drink of that cup." (1 Corinthians 11:28)
• "The cup of blessing which we bless, is it not the communion of the
  blood of Christ? The bread which we break, is it not the communion of
  the body of Christ?" (1 Corinthians 10:16)

LAYER 2
It is not a re-sacrifice of Christ; His offering was once for all
(Hebrews 10:10–14). Unworthy participation — treating it lightly while
living in known sin — brings discipline (1 Corinthians 11:27–32). Unity
of the body is pictured (1 Corinthians 10:17).

LAYER 3
Prepare by confession, reconciliation, and gratitude. Let Calvary stay
fresh in the church's memory. Look forward to the marriage supper of the
Lamb (Revelation 19:9).

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: It is a remembrance of Christ crucified — a grateful
proclamation of the finished work. Come with faith and love.

Encouragement: Love the Lamb who was slain. Keep His death central in
your worship. Trust the blood that the cup proclaims.

{DL10} ENTIRE SANCTIFICATION
================================================================
LAYER 1
Sanctification is God's will for every believer — to be set apart unto
Him and made holy in heart and life. Entire sanctification, as taught in
the holiness tradition, is a definite work of grace cleansing the heart
and enabling holy love and obedience. Without holiness no man shall see
the Lord.

Bible portions:
• "For this is the will of God, even your sanctification."
  (1 Thessalonians 4:3)
• "And the very God of peace sanctify you wholly; and I pray God your
  whole spirit and soul and body be preserved blameless unto the coming
  of our Lord Jesus Christ." (1 Thessalonians 5:23)
• "Follow peace with all men, and holiness, without which no man shall
  see the Lord." (Hebrews 12:14)
• "But as he which hath called you is holy, so be ye holy in all manner
  of conversation; Because it is written, Be ye holy; for I am holy."
  (1 Peter 1:15–16)
• "Sanctify them through thy truth: thy word is truth." (John 17:17)

LAYER 2
Justification deals with guilt; sanctification with the pollution of sin
in the heart. God promises a clean heart (Psalm 51:10; Ezekiel 36:25–27).
Faith claims the cleansing blood and the Spirit's work (Acts 15:9;
1 John 1:7). Holiness is practical — speech, habits, relationships.

LAYER 3
Sanctification is both crisis and process: a real consecration and
cleansing, then daily growth. Temptation remains; yielding to sin is not
necessary. Put off the old man; put on the new (Ephesians 4:22–24).

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: Sanctification is the Spirit's work conforming us to God's
image. Holiness is the family likeness of God's children — not optional
decoration.

Encouragement: Love the Holy One. Keep yielding every room of your heart.
Trust the Spirit to make you pure and useful for God.

{DL11} HOLY GHOST BAPTISM
================================================================
LAYER 1
The baptism with the Holy Ghost is the promise of the Father — an
enduement with power for holy living and effective witness. Jesus
commanded the disciples to wait for power from on high before they
advanced as witnesses.

Bible portions:
• "And, behold, I send the promise of my Father upon you: but tarry ye
  in the city of Jerusalem, until ye be endued with power from on high."
  (Luke 24:49)
• "But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon
  you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me." (Acts 1:8)
• "And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with
  other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance." (Acts 2:4)
• "Then laid they their hands on them, and they received the Holy Ghost."
  (Acts 8:17; see also Acts 10:44–46; 19:6)

LAYER 2
In Deeper Life teaching this is sought as a definite experience of
empowerment upon those walking in grace. The Spirit also baptises
believers into the body of Christ (1 Corinthians 12:13). Be filled
continually (Ephesians 5:18). The fruit of the Spirit marks true
fullness (Galatians 5:22–23).

LAYER 3
Power is for witness and holiness, not spiritual pride or confusion.
Ask the Father (Luke 11:13). Obey and believe. Test experiences by the
Word. The same Spirit produces purity, boldness, and love.

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: The Spirit is the promise of the Father. Power for
service is a divine gift to be received by faith and used for Christ's
glory alone.

Encouragement: Love the Spirit of truth. Keep asking, seeking, and
knocking for His fullness. Trust Him to make you a fearless, pure witness.

{DL12} REDEMPTION, HEALING AND HEALTH
================================================================
LAYER 1
Christ redeems from the penalty and power of sin through His sacrificial
death. Scripture also presents Him as the One who bore sickness and who
answers prayer for healing according to the Father's will. Believers may
ask in faith while submitting to God's wisdom.

Bible portions:
• "But he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our
  iniquities: the chastisement of our peace was upon him; and with his
  stripes we are healed." (Isaiah 53:5)
• "Who his own self bare our sins in his own body on the tree... by whose
  stripes ye were healed." (1 Peter 2:24)
• "Is any sick among you? let him call for the elders of the church; and
  let them pray over him, anointing him with oil in the name of the Lord:
  And the prayer of faith shall save the sick." (James 5:14–15)
• "Bless the LORD, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits: Who
  forgiveth all thine iniquities; who healeth all thy diseases."
  (Psalm 103:2–3)

LAYER 2
Redemption is central — the soul's salvation is the greatest healing.
Bodily healing is a mercy God often grants; it is not a guarantee that
overrides His sovereign will (2 Corinthians 12:7–9; Philippians 2:27).
Pray with faith, use wise means, and refuse bitterness if the answer
delays.

LAYER 3
Matthew 8:16–17 links Jesus' healing ministry to Isaiah's prophecy.
Continue in thanksgiving and holiness whether healed instantly, gradually,
or sustained in weakness for a higher purpose.

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: Christ is a complete Saviour. Take sins and sicknesses to
Him; measure everything by the Word and submission to God's will.

Encouragement: Love the Redeemer of your soul and body. Keep praying
without losing heart. Trust His compassion whether He heals now or holds
you until resurrection glory.

{DL13} PERSONAL EVANGELISM
================================================================
LAYER 1
Personal evangelism is every believer's God-given duty. Jesus commanded
His followers to make disciples of all nations. Mass meetings help; they
do not replace one-to-one soul-winning.

Bible portions:
• "Go ye therefore, and teach all nations..." (Matthew 28:19–20)
• "And he said unto them, Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel
  to every creature." (Mark 16:15)
• "But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you:
  and ye shall be witnesses unto me." (Acts 1:8)
• "And all things are of God, who hath reconciled us to himself by Jesus
  Christ, and hath given to us the ministry of reconciliation."
  (2 Corinthians 5:18)
• "The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life; and he that winneth souls
  is wise." (Proverbs 11:30)

LAYER 2
Andrew brought Peter; Philip found Nathanael (John 1:40–45). Daily
witness in homes and streets marked the early church (Acts 5:42). Fear of
man is a snare; love for souls casts out cowardice. Live clean so your
words have weight.

LAYER 3
Pray for the lost by name. Share your testimony. Open the Scriptures.
Invite a response of repentance and faith. Follow up new converts. Heaven
rejoices over one sinner who repents (Luke 15:7).

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: Those who have found the Messiah must invite others.
Silence is not neutrality when souls are perishing.

Encouragement: Love the lost as Christ loved you. Keep a soul on your
heart daily. Trust the Spirit to use your simple witness.

{DL14} MARRIAGE
================================================================
LAYER 1
Marriage is God's institution: one biological man and one biological
woman, joined for life. It is honourable; the bed is to be undefiled.
Polygamy and divorce-remarriage while the first companion lives contradict
the New Testament standard. Sexual purity before marriage and faithfulness
within it are required.

Bible portions:
• "Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave
  unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh." (Genesis 2:24)
• "What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder."
  (Matthew 19:6)
• "Marriage is honourable in all, and the bed undefiled: but whoremongers
  and adulterers God will judge." (Hebrews 13:4)
• "Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the church, and
  gave himself for it." (Ephesians 5:25)
• "Wives, submit yourselves unto your own husbands, as unto the Lord."
  (Ephesians 5:22)

LAYER 2
Jesus traced marriage to creation, not to culture (Matthew 19:4–8). Paul
forbids believers to be unequally yoked (2 Corinthians 6:14). Fornication
and adultery require repentance. The home should be a place of prayer,
Word, and mutual honour.

LAYER 3
Singleness is also a gift for undivided service (1 Corinthians 7:7–8, 32–35).
Whether single or married, glorify God in your body (1 Corinthians 6:18–20).

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: Marriage is God's institution. Purity adorns the doctrine
of God. Husbands love; wives submit; both fear the Lord.

Encouragement: Love God's design for the home. Keep your covenant pure.
Trust Him to strengthen every godly marriage and to keep every pure single.

{DL15} THE RAPTURE
================================================================
LAYER 1
The Rapture is the catching away of the saints — living believers and the
dead in Christ — to meet the Lord in the air. In this doctrinal tradition
it is taught as occurring before the Great Tribulation, as a blessed hope
that can take place at any time.

Bible portions:
• "For the Lord himself shall descend from heaven with a shout, with the
  voice of the archangel, and with the trump of God: and the dead in
  Christ shall rise first: Then we which are alive and remain shall be
  caught up together with them in the clouds, to meet the Lord in the air:
  and so shall we ever be with the Lord." (1 Thessalonians 4:16–17)
• "In a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trump: for the
  trumpet shall sound, and the dead shall be raised incorruptible, and we
  shall be changed." (1 Corinthians 15:51–52)
• "And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again, and receive
  you unto myself; that where I am, there ye may be also." (John 14:3)
• "Looking for that blessed hope, and the glorious appearing of the great
  God and our Saviour Jesus Christ." (Titus 2:13)

LAYER 2
This hope comforts mourners (1 Thessalonians 4:18) and purifies the living
(1 John 3:2–3). Believers are not appointed to wrath (1 Thessalonians 5:9).
Be ready; do not sleep spiritually (1 Thessalonians 5:6).

LAYER 3
Exact charts differ among teachers; the clear duty is holiness and
watchfulness. Occupy until He comes (Luke 19:13). Encourage one another
with these words.

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry on 1 Thessalonians 4: The coming of the Lord is the comfort
of mourners and the hope of the church. Be ready.

Encouragement: Love His appearing. Keep oil in your lamp. Trust that He
will not forget one of His own when the trumpet sounds.

{DL16} THE RESURRECTION OF THE DEAD
================================================================
LAYER 1
The Bible teaches the bodily resurrection of both the just and the unjust.
Christ's resurrection is the firstfruits and the guarantee. Death is not
the end of the person.

Bible portions:
• "Marvel not at this: for the hour is coming, in the which all that are
  in the graves shall hear his voice, And shall come forth; they that have
  done good, unto the resurrection of life; and they that have done evil,
  unto the resurrection of damnation." (John 5:28–29)
• "And many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some
  to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt."
  (Daniel 12:2)
• "But now is Christ risen from the dead, and become the firstfruits of
  them that slept." (1 Corinthians 15:20)
• "And have hope toward God... that there shall be a resurrection of the
  dead, both of the just and unjust." (Acts 24:15)

LAYER 2
The believer's body is sown in weakness and raised in power
(1 Corinthians 15:42–44). We shall be like Him (Philippians 3:20–21;
1 John 3:2). Therefore labour is not in vain in the Lord
(1 Corinthians 15:58).

LAYER 3
Deny the resurrection and you empty the gospel (1 Corinthians 15:12–19).
Comfort one another with the hope of reunion and glory.

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: The same body sown in weakness shall be raised in power.
Live and serve in hope of that day.

Encouragement: Love the risen Christ. Keep serving while it is day. Trust
God with your body in life and in the grave.

{DL17} THE GREAT TRIBULATION
================================================================
LAYER 1
The Great Tribulation is a future period of unprecedented trouble on the
earth, spoken of by the prophets and by Jesus. It involves deception,
judgment, persecution, and the rise of wicked power before the open reign
of Christ.

Bible portions:
• "For then shall be great tribulation, such as was not since the beginning
  of the world to this time, no, nor ever shall be." (Matthew 24:21)
• "And at that time shall Michael stand up... and there shall be a time of
  trouble, such as never was since there was a nation even to that same
  time." (Daniel 12:1)
• "Alas! for that day is great, so that none is like it: it is even the
  time of Jacob's trouble." (Jeremiah 30:7)
• See also Revelation chapters 6–19 for the unfolding of judgments.

LAYER 2
Jesus said those days would be shortened for the elect's sake
(Matthew 24:22). False christs and false prophets will show great signs
(Matthew 24:24). The call in every age is endurance and faithfulness.

LAYER 3
Do not be terrified into unbelief; be steadfast. Present tribulations are
real, but the future climax will exceed them. Keep preaching the gospel
while the door is open.

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry on Matthew 24: Christ foretold troubles so His people would
not be surprised, but prepared — steadfast, not driven from faith.

Encouragement: Love the Lord who holds history. Keep watch and pray. Trust
Him to keep His own through every storm.

{DL18} THE SECOND COMING OF CHRIST
================================================================
LAYER 1
The same Jesus who ascended will return personally, visibly, and in power
and glory. Every eye shall see Him. He comes to judge, to reign, and to
complete redemption's public victory.

Bible portions:
• "This same Jesus, which is taken up from you into heaven, shall so come
  in like manner as ye have seen him go into heaven." (Acts 1:11)
• "Behold, he cometh with clouds; and every eye shall see him."
  (Revelation 1:7)
• "And then shall appear the sign of the Son of man in heaven... and they
  shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds of heaven with power and
  great glory." (Matthew 24:30)
• "When the Son of man shall come in his glory, and all the holy angels
  with him, then shall he sit upon the throne of his glory."
  (Matthew 25:31)

LAYER 2
His coming is certain, public, and decisive. The wicked will mourn; the
righteous will rejoice. Live as servants who expect their Lord's return
(Luke 12:35–40).

LAYER 3
Related to but distinct in emphasis from the catching away of the church
(DL15). Hold both the blessed hope and the glorious appearing with
reverence. Refuse date-setting (Matthew 24:36).

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: The second coming is certain and decisive. Therefore live
ready every day.

Encouragement: Love the coming King. Keep your life ready for inspection.
Trust that He who promised will come and will not tarry beyond the
Father's time.

================================================================
{DL19} CHRIST'S MILLENNIAL REIGN
================================================================
LAYER 1
Scripture teaches a thousand-year reign of Christ with His people, during
which Satan is bound. The prophets foresaw worldwide peace under Messiah's
rule. The Lord shall be king over all the earth.

Bible portions:
• "And he laid hold on the dragon, that old serpent, which is the Devil,
  and Satan, and bound him a thousand years... and they lived and reigned
  with Christ a thousand years." (Revelation 20:2, 4)
• "And the LORD shall be king over all the earth: in that day shall there
  be one LORD, and his name one." (Zechariah 14:9)
• "The wolf also shall dwell with the lamb... They shall not hurt nor
  destroy in all my holy mountain: for the earth shall be full of the
  knowledge of the LORD." (Isaiah 11:6, 9)
• "And the kingdom and dominion... shall be given to the people of the
  saints of the most High." (Daniel 7:27)

LAYER 2
Isaiah 2:2–4 pictures nations learning God's law and refusing war. This
reign is not the final eternal state; after it come final judgment and the
new creation (Revelation 20–22). Faithful service now prepares for then.

LAYER 3
Matthew Henry: Whatever the exact scheme of times, the kingdoms of this
world shall become the kingdoms of our Lord and of His Christ.

LAYER 4
Live as a citizen of that coming kingdom — righteous, peaceful, and loyal
to the King already.

Encouragement: Love the reigning Christ. Keep serving in the small trusts
He gives now. Trust that history ends in His victory, not chaos.

{DL20} THE GREAT WHITE THRONE JUDGMENT
================================================================
LAYER 1
After the millennial scene, the dead small and great stand before God at
the Great White Throne. Books are opened; the book of life is opened.
Anyone not found written in the book of life is cast into the lake of fire.

Bible portions:
• "And I saw a great white throne, and him that sat on it... And I saw the
  dead, small and great, stand before God; and the books were opened: and
  another book was opened, which is the book of life: and the dead were
  judged out of those things which were written in the books, according to
  their works." (Revelation 20:11–12)
• "And whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into
  the lake of fire." (Revelation 20:15)
• "And as it is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the
  judgment." (Hebrews 9:27)
• "For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ." 
  (2 Corinthians 5:10 — believers' assessment; the White Throne centres on
  the unsaved dead)

LAYER 2
No appeal remains on that day. Works are evidence; the decisive issue is
whether one is in the book of life — that is, in Christ by faith. Flee to
Him now (John 3:16–18; 5:24).

LAYER 3
Matthew Henry: Judgment is certain and universal. The only way to stand is
to be found in Christ now, with sins washed in His blood.

Encouragement: Love the Saviour who saves from that wrath. Keep your life
hidden in Him. Trust His promise that whoever believes shall not come into
condemnation.

{DL21} THE NEW HEAVEN AND THE NEW EARTH
================================================================
LAYER 1
God's final purpose is a new heaven and a new earth where righteousness
dwells. God will dwell with His people. There will be no more death,
sorrow, crying, or pain. The redeemed shall see His face.

Bible portions:
• "And I saw a new heaven and a new earth: for the first heaven and the
  first earth were passed away." (Revelation 21:1)
• "And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be
  no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any
  more pain: for the former things are passed away." (Revelation 21:4)
• "Nevertheless we, according to his promise, look for new heavens and a
  new earth, wherein dwelleth righteousness." (2 Peter 3:13)
• "And they shall see his face; and his name shall be in their foreheads."
  (Revelation 22:4)

LAYER 2
The holy city, New Jerusalem, is described in glory (Revelation 21–22).
The curse is gone (Revelation 22:3). This is Eden restored and surpassed —
not escape from creation, but creation renewed under God.

LAYER 3
Matthew Henry: The end of the believer's journey is not shadows but
substance — God Himself with His people in everlasting joy.

Encouragement: Love the God who makes all things new. Keep your hope fixed
beyond this passing world. Trust every tear to the day He wipes them away.

================================================================
{DL22} HELL FIRE
================================================================
LAYER 1
Hell fire is the eternal punishment of the devil, his angels, and all who
refuse Christ's salvation. It is real, just, and everlasting. The warning
is given so sinners will flee to the Saviour today.

Bible portions:
• "Then shall he say also unto them on the left hand, Depart from me, ye
  cursed, into everlasting fire, prepared for the devil and his angels."
  (Matthew 25:41)
• "And these shall go away into everlasting punishment: but the righteous
  into life eternal." (Matthew 25:46)
• "And the devil that deceived them was cast into the lake of fire and
  brimstone... and shall be tormented day and night for ever and ever."
  (Revelation 20:10)
• "But the fearful, and unbelieving, and the abominable, and murderers, and
  whoremongers, and sorcerers, and idolaters, and all liars, shall have
  their part in the lake which burneth with fire and brimstone: which is
  the second death." (Revelation 21:8)
• "And fear not them which kill the body, but are not able to kill the
  soul: but rather fear him which is able to destroy both soul and body in
  hell." (Matthew 10:28)

LAYER 2
Jesus spoke often of Gehenna and outer darkness. The lake of fire is the
final second death (Revelation 20:14–15). Hades is intermediate; the lake
of fire is final. The door of mercy is open now (2 Corinthians 6:2).

LAYER 3
Preach hell as Scripture does — soberly, to lead to repentance and faith,
not to harden or entertain. The love of Christ constrains urgent witness.

LAYER 4
Matthew Henry: The wrath to come is avoided only in Christ. Today if you
hear His voice, harden not your heart.

Encouragement: Love the God who warns because He loves. Keep pointing
sinners to the cross. Trust the blood of Jesus — the only escape.

================================================================
Whenever you finish a doctrine, remind the hearer:
"Love the Lord your God with all your heart. Keep a warm, obedient
relationship with Him through the Word and prayer. Trust Him with your
life and your eternity — He is faithful, and Jesus saves to the
uttermost."

Seal verses:
• Deuteronomy 6:5 — Love the LORD thy God with all thine heart.
• Proverbs 3:5 — Trust in the LORD with all thine heart.
• James 4:8 — Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you.
• Hebrews 7:25 — He is able also to save them to the uttermost that come
  unto God by him.

# Simple finance books

Procurement stays on the existing ERP screens. Money moves only after approval.

1. Chart of accounts — cash, expense, asset, liability, equity, receivable, payable (income is included for grants).
2. Project codes.
3. Expense codes — each one must use one project and one account.
4. Budget codes — each one must use one project and one expense code.
5. Inputs: payment request, procurement invoice, or a journal.
6. Approval: submitted → program approved → finance approved → paid.
7. Finance approval posts debit expense / credit accounts payable.
8. Paid posts debit accounts payable / credit cash.
9. Those lines are the account ledger and the general ledger.
10. Trial balance, financial statements, project report and variance read those lines.
11. A balanced journal posts immediately.
12. Bank reconciliation ticks cash-book lines against a statement balance.

Open Finance books after login. Send a procurement invoice from ERP → Invoices.
